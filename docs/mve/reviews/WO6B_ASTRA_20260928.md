## v2 contract and interpretation

`grounded.py`, `config/grounded_v2.json`, and
`schemas/mve_grounded_card_v2.json` define a separate v2 envelope. The embedded
WO-2 hypothesis remains a valid v1 card, always `preliminary`. Envelope status is
`grounded`, `ungrounded`, `contradictory`, or `underpowered`. Malformed/empty slots
have no card. Thus older readers remain supported without extending v1's lifecycle
or allowing these diagnostics to authorize a handoff.

Each perception response is `{observations:[{id,feature_tag,region,text}]}` with
at most three observations. IDs are `p0:1`…`p0:3` or `p1:1`…`p1:3`, unique per pass,
scoped to this job. Text is nonempty, at most 80 characters; each compact observation
is at most 190 UTF-8 bytes. Regions are `whole` or the nine top/middle/bottom ×
left/center/right cells. Tags are the versioned closed vocabulary:
`histogram_peak`, `histogram_tail`, `spacing_gap`, `point_cluster`, `void`,
`symmetry_axis`, `spiral_arm`, `curve_shape`, `density_gradient`, `intersection`.
No distribution names are perception tags. The two fresh-context perception
prompts differ only in their ID namespace. The third call sees the same blinded
pixels plus exactly this job's validated observations, within 2,048 prompt bytes.

Cards retain v1 proposal fields and add `observation_ids`. Missing, empty or
entirely invalid citations mean **ungrounded**, not malformed. At least one valid
local citation establishes syntactic grounding only: it does not verify a pixel
feature, the claim, or the relevance of that feature. The envelope binds the job,
observations, proposal, v1 hypothesis, assessments and check through its digest;
the validator also checks proposal/hypothesis consistency and citation bindings.

The pinned exclusive table currently contains **GUE versus Poisson** for the same
spacing data set, restricted to KS spacing assertions. Both members are marked
contradictory, regardless of their citation status. Explicit finite aliases cover
WO-6's central-square “distances”/“spacings” wording. It does not infer arbitrary
paraphrases or mark a pair-correlation comparison as a spacing assertion. The v2
prompt defines baseline as the distribution asserted to fit, not the opponent.

Feature repeatability is Jaccard of the two sets of tags. Two empty sets or a
missing/invalid pass yield unavailable, not perfect agreement. Report each job
and the unweighted mean of available jobs within each arm, with the valid-pair
count. All repeatability is **descriptive; agreement verifies nothing**.

Counts are overlapping diagnostics: an uncited contradictory card counts as both
ungrounded and contradictory; a grounded card may also be underpowered. Display
precedence is malformed, contradictory, ungrounded, underpowered, grounded.
The only mapped checks are `gaudin_ks` / `Gaudin GUE` and `poisson_ks` / `Poisson`,
with `direction=greater`. Data must be exactly `source_gaps` for the spectral/Dyson
histograms or `source_index_gaps` for prime indices. Spatial/radial pixel distances,
histogram bin heights and unimplemented pair correlation remain unavailable.
These diagnostics never silently substitute index spacings for spatial distances.

## Power before claims

`python3 -m mve.observer.power --output <new-file.json>` is pure NumPy/SciPy, seed
**2026092806**, 256 independent calibration draws and 128 independent evaluation
draws per population/module. The fixed grid is 16, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512, 1024,
plus both actual snapshot gap counts. `config/wo6b_power.json` pins all rows,
critical values, empirical sizes, Wilson 95% Monte Carlo intervals and grid minima.

GUE reference samples use independent GUE(64) tridiagonal bulk eigenvalues, central
60%, semicircle unfolding. Poisson uses exponential gaps. Native controls port
the exact pinned WO-1 algorithms and mulberry32 draw order, with SciPy replacing
Sturm bisection. Spectral keeps the first 89 of the eight-matrix normalized gaps;
Dyson keeps eight 200-dimensional matrices' 120 central levels; Ulam draws once
for every integer 1…30000; sphere draws once for every integer 3…100000. The last
two distinct RNG offsets are preserved. All committed null arrays reproduce:
integer arrays exactly; spectral error <3e-14; Dyson error <1.8e-13.

Dyson spacings are computed within segments, with per-segment mean normalization;
no gap is formed across segment boundaries. Prime index gaps are unfolded by
`log(midpoint index)`. All diagnostic samples are finally normalized to mean one.
Small grids use prefixes; larger grids concatenate independent native batches,
never padding a fixed image or counting repeated draws as new discovery clusters.
The true reference is finite GUE, not a guarantee of infinite Gaudin accuracy.

**Stage-1 admission uses the upper empirical rank tail at alpha .05**, matching
`greater`, and takes the minimum power over both alternative populations. Below
0.8, or without a matching power row at the card's exact n, the check is
`underpowered`; the hypothesis stays preliminary. Even above 0.8 it is only
`stage1_only`: no replication, calibrated survival verdict, or GO2 claim. The
model's own discrepancy threshold remains hashed, but this power screen does not
validate that threshold as a calibrated inferential kill rule.

## WO-1b for GO2 — design only (one page)

Freeze **20 ordinary clusters across four strata, five per module**, plus **10
contrast clusters** balanced across at least two strata. Each ordinary cluster
needs mutually disjoint discovery D, replication R and same-stratum donor blocks;
no block, interval or seed is reused, and current development data never enter
these pools. For zero modules allocate non-overlapping zero-index intervals at
specified heights, with independent null seeds. For primes allocate disjoint
integer-index intervals, not several renders of the same range. Pin support,
population, role, generator, stratum, data digest, crop, camera and V=2/C=3 before
opening outcomes. The current renderer needs interval-selection/export support;
changing only seed/camera cannot create disjoint real blocks.

Match each null's count, stratum, module parameters and rendering to its real
block. In particular fix Dyson's current 999-versus-952 gap mismatch. Select the
shuffled donor from unused blocks in the same stratum by one frozen seed; check
its cards on the recipient's D then R. Keep R untouched by observer/prompt tuning.
Include Poisson/GOE spacing contrasts and explicitly specified arithmetic/geometry
contrasts for prime strata, with separate D/R and independent seeds. Contrasts
must target implemented, powered checks; an arbitrary random picture is not a
sensitivity control. Freeze family/check mappings and require at least 8/10
contrast detections. K>=20 alone does not establish overall GO2 power or A1/A2.

**Inventory:** 20 × (D, R, donor, null-D, null-R) = 100 source blocks. The existing
WO-4 allocator also requires null-D/null-R twins for contrast clusters: ten × four
blocks = 40 more. With two views, **280 prospective snapshots**, plus the eight
existing development snapshots kept separate; repeat verification renders 560
prospective images. If the contrast schema later drops unused contrast nulls,
that is a separate reviewed change, reducing the prospective inventory to 240.
Neither the current eight snapshots nor more views of them meet this requirement.

**Disk estimate:** the current eight blinded PNG+data exports total 554,003 bytes;
linear scaling suggests ~19.4 MB for 280 such retained exports, or ~38.8 MB for two
passes. Full-page/working capture evidence is much larger: WO-1's 7,583,370-byte
receipt for eight snapshots extrapolates to ~265 MB, already above the current

both blinded passes and data/digest/difference receipts, with an explicit reviewed
