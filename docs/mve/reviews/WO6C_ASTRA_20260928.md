## Comparative protocol and data boundary

Eight jobs: one real/null pair and one independent new-null/new-null pair for each
of spectral, field-dyson, polar-ulam and space-08. Each job gets two fresh perception
calls and one card call, three card slots, zero retries: **24 calls / 24 slots**.
One **1024×352 RGB composite PNG** is sent per request. Its two crops carry only
`A` and `B`. Seed **63001000** fixes order. Source identities, module, pair type,
and the side permutation are recorded only in the sealed local design; the model
receives the pixels, the generic prompt, and on call three this job's observations.
There is no module name, source label, seed or arm in the prompt. Sealed here means
withheld from transport and written locally with mode 0600, not encrypted storage.

Perception asks “What differs between A and B?” and returns at most three
`{id, side, feature_tag, region, text}` objects per pass. IDs are `p0:1`…`p1:3`;
side is A, B or both; region is whole or a fixed nine-cell region; text ≤64 chars.
Cards v3 contain `{claim, side, feature_tag, data, observation_ids}`. Side must be
A or B and mean **more** of the declared feature. Every citation must resolve to
this job and match both the card's side and feature tag. A `both` observation cannot
ground a directional claim. Empty observations/cards are permitted. Schemas and
hashed envelopes bind proposal, observations, check and preliminary status.

Only the eight hard-allowlisted WO-1 fixture paths are accepted. New capture seeds
are **63000000–63000007**, registered in `config/wo6c_development.json`, with
numerical hashes and development-only roles. Study seeds reserve
**[63100000,63200000)**. Code asserts no collision with WO-1b's 140 block seeds
28000000–28000139, power/donor roots 2026092816/2026092817, or its source intervals.
New prime nulls stay on native development support [1,30001) or [1,100001), disjoint
from WO-1b's integer envelope [200001,2840001). Original development zero indices
through 1000 are disjoint from every prospective zero interval. Matrix nulls have
no arithmetic source interval. Native matrix/prime algorithms and RNG draw order
are preserved. No prospective manifest data, D/R/donor/null/contrast numerical block,
Odlyzko cache or prospective generated artifact was opened for development.

## Checks and power

All checks consume exported numbers, never pixels. `source_gaps` means the actual
exported spectral gaps (no extra mean normalization), or within-segment Dyson gaps
normalized per segment exactly as the renderer does. No cross-segment gap is formed.
`display_coordinates` is computed from exported indices: native Ulam coordinates
on its fixed ring scale; sphere cone coordinates with scale 1.1/N, `toDisplay`,
camera [.55,.45,3.5], zero target, vertical-up basis and 42-degree perspective FOV.
No image extraction, fitted camera, invented spatial distances or index-gap
substitution is used. Checks are whole-sample diagnostics; a local observation's
citation alone does not prove a whole-sample claim.

| Family | Frozen statistic | One sample | A versus B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Linear/radial density | Absolute Spearman correlation of equal-volume bin counts with x, y or distance from fixed center; 16 bins per axis, empty bins retained. Radial counts are **not** uncorrected shell totals. | Native-null upper empirical rank tail | Absolute difference of the two scores; side with larger gradient magnitude |
| Modality | Binned Gaussian KDE: 128 bins on fixed support, sigma=6 bins, zero padding; modes have prominence ≥10% of maximum and separation ≥12 bins | Native-null upper rank tail of mode count | Absolute difference in counts; side with more modes |
| Reflection | 1 − total variation between fixed-axis histogram and its reflection: 128 bins for 1D, **joint 32×32** cells for spatial data | Native-null upper rank tail (closer symmetry) | Absolute score difference; side with closer symmetry |
| Spacing KS | Empirical two-sample KS on histogram source spacings | One observed sample against a pinned independent native reference, then native calibration | KS(A,B); side determined by CDF sign at maximum absolute discrepancy (“more large spacings” there), not a stochastic-dominance claim |

Histogram support is [0,3]; spatial support is [-1,1] in normalized display units.
The theoretical GUE/Poisson one-sample assertions from WO-6b are not used as
comparative evidence: spacing_ks compares the two empirical spacing samples.

`power.comparative_study()` is pinned to seed 63100000, **64 calibration draws and
32 held-out evaluation draws**, alpha .05, at every required development n/pair n.
Spatial draws condition the exact native Bernoulli generator on total n by rejection
on **count only**, never features. One uninterrupted Mulberry stream supplies
independent accepted samples per count. Histogram draws use independent registered
native batches, prefixing/concatenating within-segment spacings when needed.
Calibration/evaluation/reference samples are disjoint; rows reuse pools across
features, so feature-row Monte Carlo errors are not independent.

Sensitivity alternatives are fixed numerical effects applied only to newly simulated
native draws: density coordinate concentrated by fourth-power quantiles; radial
contraction by 0.25; two narrow lobes at ±0.28 of support width for modality; mirrored
paired points for reflection; spacing scale ×1.7 for KS. These alternatives are
not fitted to observed real/null outcomes or rendered as discovery evidence.
Power is **for these specified effects**, not arbitrary claims, all effect sizes,
or real-versus-null discrimination. A card with matched two-sample power <0.8 is
underpowered. Low powers are retained, not tuned away.

Monte Carlo p=(1+# calibration statistics ≥ observed)/(65). Ties are conservative.
The table's empirical null size is descriptive, not guaranteed conditional size;
there is no multiplicity correction and no GO decision. Wilson 95% power intervals
and all 107 rows are committed in `config/wo6c_power.json`. Build took **73.82 s**;
an independent run reproduced the file byte for byte. A power of 1 from 32/32 has
Wilson interval [0.892821,1], not certainty.

## Scoring and WO-6b re-score

A card is **separating** only when its local citations match its named side/tag,
its data field maps exactly to an implemented two-sample check, matched power is
≥0.8, p≤.05, and the unblinded numeric result agrees with the claimed side. Powered
significant disagreement is **wrong-side**. No significant difference or unavailable
mapping is **non-separating**; failed grounding/malformed slots are **ungrounded**;
matched power below .8 is **underpowered**. These are exclusive display categories,
with grounding checked before power and significance. Empty/failed allocated slots
remain non-separating with their reason. Every card stays preliminary.

On null/null pairs, every proposed textual difference claim is false by design,
including malformed, ungrounded or numerically non-separating claims. The report's
false-difference rate divides that claim count by **all 12 allocated null/null
slots**, not just checked cards. It separately reports the separating false-difference
rate. This is an opportunity-level descriptive rate, not a GO2 survival statistic.

`WO6C_RESCORE_20260928.json` reads the committed WO-6b live report without changing
anything under `docs/mve/reviews/wo6b-live/`. It applies explicit v2→v3 candidate-tag
translations and exclusions. **11/24** cards explicitly name exported source_gaps,
so each receives the five available one-sample numerical diagnostics (55 results).
**13/24 remain unavailable**: pixel/visible-point coordinates, an unspecified profile,
left-panel curves, or wireframe coordinates cannot be silently mapped to the pinned
number fields. These extra diagnostics do **not** rewrite or validate the original
statistic/direction/baseline. No old card has comparative side evidence; **0/24 become
comparative cards or survivors**. All 24 preserve their historical claims.
