## WO-4

- `mve/observer/design.py` consumes the WO-1 manifest shape. The frozen config
  contains module × family strata; separate development, discovery, replication,
  donor and contrast pools; family check templates; the image-free library; V/C;
  retries; and one allocation seed. Allocation permutes the role pools, pairs
  D/R blocks, exclusively assigns donors, records the pre-rendered baseline seeds,
  and shuffles opaque job IDs. Every ordinary cluster gets all four arms.
- Ordinary runs use V=2, C=3, S=6; pilot runs use V=1, C=3, S=3. Contrasts have
  separate D/R blocks. Disjoint support intervals, source hashes, role consistency,
  exact view counts and unique baseline seeds are checked. Support declarations
  do not prove statistical independence. Actual WO-1 development captures are
  **not promoted** to discovery/replication inputs.
- `snapshots.validate_manifest` retains its original defaults. Its explicit
  prospective mode permits the additional roles and `checkable` state.
  Replication source bytes are deferred until the frozen card's stage-2 loader;
  their declared identity/path is validated earlier. A test forbids opening those
  files during allocation. Stage 2 verifies the actual bytes against the frozen hash.
- `run.py` accepts only the exact immutable local `Replay` type. DeepSeek uses the
  unchanged WP-3 **two-call geometric perceiver**, retaining its raw replies and
  alignment/instability receipt, followed by a fresh-context card request through
  WP-0b. This is three logical calls per visual view, matching §7's cost accounting;
  it does not pretend that WP-3's geometric parser already implements WO-2 cards.
  Each dispatch uses phase P1, verified prices, the off-peak policy, a fresh nonce,
  and the exact `FakeTransport` boundary. There is no live mode or live flag.
- All possible attempt ceilings, including up to two retries, are reserved in the
  ledger **before the first dispatch**. A reservation ticket is replaced by the
  WP-0b per-attempt reservation just before dispatch. Unused tickets are cancelled;
  unknown charges remain reserved. A worktree lock enforces one WO-4/5 writer.
  The entire worst case must fit USD 0.25, P1 and aggregate remaining budgets.
- The runner requires a design hash in `packets.WO-4.frozen_design_sha256s`, checks
  the allocation again, and requires reconciliation tied to the exact current
  ledger snapshot, matching usage rows, their hash, known spend and remaining
  funds. Fixture reconciliation is explicitly synthetic; **this packet does not
  reconcile the real campaign or assert its remaining budget**.
- The card call authors claim/prediction/resemblance fields within the frozen
  check mapping. Host code binds sources, observer and check partitions, then uses
  the WO-2 API to freeze and the WO-3 runner for both stages. No replacement check
  is selected after a failure. The WO-2 `development` check field denotes the
  cluster's discovery block, not the separate prompt-development pool.
- Image-free cards preserve their frozen library author's identity. They make no
  model call and receive no image. GO1 reports that origin separately while keeping
  all S shared-control slots in the observer-run denominator. The PNG reference
  on these cards is source provenance only. It is not claimed as a delivered image.
- Empty, malformed, refused, failed and inconclusive slots remain in denominators.
  GO2 uses integer survivor-count differences (common S cancels), all 2^K flips for
  K≤20, and 100,000 seeded flips otherwise: `(1+r)/(B+1)` plus a one-sided 95% upper
  bound. It reports lift, per-stratum comparisons, pooled/per-stratum nominal CP
  intervals, and A1/A2 assumptions. Conditional success requires all three p≤.05,
  K≥20, ≥2 strata, and at least 8 detected contrast clusters out of exactly 10.
  A contrast detection means at least one baseline-exceeding survivor in its slots.
  Smaller runs are descriptive only. Replay results never certify a scientific gate.
- O-M2 refuses with `owner Q2 unanswered`. Human intake validates through `Card`,
  reports GO1 and remains excluded from GO2 pending Q6. The owner-question fields
  are explicit null placeholders in `mve/observer/config/owner_questions.json`.
  Editing those placeholders cannot enable hosted calls or relay.

Only a **one-cluster synthetic fixture** is frozen in DEPS.lock. Its small numerical

## WO-5

`Outbox` has no configurable external destination. It writes only under
`mve/generated/outbox/{exchange,atlas}`. Paths and symlinks are checked, files are
exclusive/immutable, and both schemas and artifact hashes are validated.

- Exchange drafts contain the C4 live key shape, stable `mve-card-<card_id>` work ID,
  revision/round lineage, report SHA, and the live protocol's UTF-8 canonical
  `packet_hash` algorithm (`ensure_ascii=False`). Current survivor plus current
  human adoption is required. Round 2 supersedes round 1 and verifies its card
  lineage; stale, skipped or missing lineage is refused.
- Draft `packet_type` is C4's `attack_lane_proposal`. The surveyed live submit CLI
  itself emits `submission` and requires a report at the specified commit.
  An ignored outbox report is not yet published at that commit. These are reviewable
  drafts, **not claims of accepted live submissions**; Opus must resolve publication
  and relay details under Q3/Q4/Q5 before any eventual relay.
- Atlas result bundles use a byte-exact vendored schema, checked against its pinned
  SHA on every validation. Bundle artifacts and sources must exist under the local
  atlas-shaped staging root and match hashes. Review is always unreviewed, and
  `reproduce` is recorded without execution. New outcomes need a revision-bound
  lane receipt. Baseline-explained/replicated observations never create lane proposals.
- Status mapping is labelled **proposal pending atlas-owner confirmation (Q5)**.
  `technical_failure` is refused before any bundle/artifact write because of the
  `services.py:229` staging defect. `relay()` always refuses; it reports missing
  Q3/Q4/Q5 answers without implementing a transport.
- H0 → `inconclusive` and H1 → `completed` bundles were written and validated at
  `mve/generated/outbox/atlas/inbox/wo45-reviewed-H0.json` and `wo45-reviewed-H1.json`. No real card
  was eligible for an exchange submission; round/supersession fixtures use a
  disposable test outbox.
