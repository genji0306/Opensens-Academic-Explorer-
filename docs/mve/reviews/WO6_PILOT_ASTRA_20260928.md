## Delivered scope and the source-data shortfall

The activation is a **reduced 24-call descriptive pilot**, not the complete
102-call, ten-independent-cluster experiment in plan r6 §5. The reviewed WO-1
capture supplies four development-only real/null pairs, eight images total.
Those catalogues overlap, and supply **zero independent discovery/replication
pairs, zero same-stratum donors, and zero contrast clusters**. They have not been
renamed or duplicated to manufacture independent blocks. This shortfall was
raised during the build; the smaller run is the stated default assumption, not
a new owner answer. Opus must assess this reduced scope when reviewing activation.

The four modules are spectral (05), field-dyson, polar-ulam, and space-08 prime
sphere, each with its reviewed control-only twin. All four arms have three slots
per module, with fixed seeded order, V=1, S=3, no retries, no topping up:

- Real and null-twin: eight blinded images × three calls = **24 calls**. The first
  two requests are byte-identical fresh-context perception requests; the third
  independently requests at most three cards. Agreement establishes nothing.
- Image-free: twelve frozen family-specific generic cards (Gaudin GUE for spacings, Cramér for primes), attributed to `model:astra-library`,
  with an explicit offline library reference and no hosted call. They remain a
  separate origin in GO1 and do not masquerade as owner input or a second observer.
- Shuffled: twelve `unavailable_donor` slots retained in the denominator, no image
  substituted and no call. No untouched replication or contrast is claimed.
- O-M2: **SKIPPED — owner Q2 unanswered**. No owner cards are inferred.

GO2 is forced descriptive-only and false. Four *development pairs* are reported
separately from zero independent clusters. All-zero slot accounting gives exact
sign-flip p=1 over 16 patterns for each comparison; this is explicitly not a
scientific comparison given the missing blocks and unestablished A1/A2. Nominal
slot intervals are labelled descriptive. GO3/GO4 remain not established.
The full §5 experiment requires a separate reviewed source-data allocation.
At this packet's conservative allowance, 102 calls would reserve USD 0.626688,
exceeding the owner-approved USD 0.25 reservation ceiling.

This cut names WP-0b as the sole prior hosted attempt, consistent with the supplied
review record. It is reconciliation of provider **usage**, not a vendor invoice or
an online account balance. Other campaign spending requires a new reviewed cut;
this isolated pilot does not coordinate concurrent spend in unrelated processes.

The input allowance covers each measured, resized lab crop, at most 2,048 UTF-8

**not a vendor-verified image-token formula or a server-enforced input cap**.
The only measured vendor example remains 236 input tokens on WP-0b's 288×288 PNG.
Actual usage over the allowance is recorded without clamping and freezes further
calls. Unknown usage retains the entire reservation; it is never zero-filled.

Prices/window reuse the owner-supplied, Opus-verified evidence dated **2026-09-27**.
No dispatch-date vendor lookup or new price verification was performed in this
network-off build. The source hash is verified before dispatch; a price update
needs another reviewed packet. Chinese holidays receive no peak exemption.

Every attempted exchange goes through reviewed WP-0b `prepare_dispatch`,
`prepare_call`, `receive_live`, and WP-9a P1 reserve/mark-dispatched/reconcile.
Peak refusal is rechecked after call-time Keychain preparation and immediately
before the sole exchange. The host remains fixed; no proxy, redirect, retry or SDK
is added. The `oae-deepseek-api-key` value is never persisted or printed; echoed
bytes are scrubbed before raw storage. Raw bytes precede parsing, with the reviewed
1 MiB reply cap. Malformed replies/requests retain failed slots. Process loss leaves
uncertain durable reservations and a non-restartable live run for Opus to reconcile.
