Prime blocks reserve disjoint 40,000-integer windows: polar-ulam collectively
[200001,1520001), space-08 [1520001,2840001). Each takes 2,049 indices, hence 2,048
gaps. Real blocks use the first arrivals from a local sieve. Random-prime twins
and Cramér contrasts use the existing native Mulberry Bernoulli 1/log(integer)
rule, extended to their reserved interval, taking the first 2,049 arrivals.
Insufficient arrivals refuse rather than top up. Shuffled-index contrasts uniformly
permute site membership within their own window and take a fixed-count subset;
this is not merely reordering an unchanged list of gaps.

Prime drawings use local index `i - window_start + 1`, extent 40,000, with the
absolute source indices, index origin and locally unfolded gaps also exported.
The check targets **source-index spacings**, not Ulam or sphere pixel distances.
This is a declared local-coordinate visualization of each disjoint arithmetic
block; it is not the native visualization of all primes starting at 1.

Zero twins use finite beta=2 tridiagonal bulk spectra: dimension 64 for spectral,
200 for Dyson; contrasts use beta=1 or exponential spacings. Only within-matrix
bulk gaps are collected, to the identical 1,024-gap count, then normalized to mean
one. Dyson draws cumulative levels built from those gaps as one segment, identically
for every role; this joins independent matrix gap chunks into a synthetic process.
The sensitivity calculation uses that same construction. No pair-correlation
power or claim is attached to the native auxiliary pair-correlation panel.

The zero null twins are **positive GUE controls**, never negative examples of GUE.
The prime Cramér contrast and random-prime twin deliberately share a generator
family but have separate windows/seeds. Prime contrasts test the declared GUE-gap
claim's sensitivity; they do not establish prime-versus-random-prime discrimination
or spatial sensitivity. Power is checked at the exact block n. Prime simulations
use the declared 40,000-site recipe at [200001,240001), with mean-one local-log
unfolding; power at every possible integer height is not separately claimed.

`power.prospective_check()` implements the frozen upper-tail KS diagnostic and
checks n and the matching powered row. It returns `stage1_sensitivity_only`, with
`inferential=false`. The nominal .05 calibration rank is not a guarantee of
conditional size: the empirical sizes above are reported explicitly. No observed
real or R block statistics were used to choose thresholds. This packet does not
extend WO-4's existing zero-only inferential allocator to prime inferential checks,
freeze future model prompts, authorize hosted calls, or claim any GO gate.
