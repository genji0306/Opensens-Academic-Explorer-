## Delivered

- **WO-2:** independent `oae-mve-hypothesis-card-v1` JSON Schema; immutable card
  validation; canonical full-specification and check-specification hashes;
  `frozen` / `preliminary` lifecycle; revision-bound judgments, human-only
  adoption, current-survivor handoff; transitive evidence invalidation through
  `mve.graph.downstream`. Model judgments have weight 0.5 and cannot adopt.
- **Formal boundary:** `hyp_N` is rejected directly and through dependency
  closure in problem propositions, formal authorization, assumptions,
  derivations and IR building. Raw IR emission and proposition-payload building
  also reject hypothesis references, including `source_refs` and `depends_on`.
  No hypothesis collection was added to schema v5.
- **GO1:** per-observer denominator is requested views × requested slots;
  missing, refused, malformed, failed and wrong-observer replies remain failed
  slots. Retries replace replies, not opportunities. Human checkability is keyed
  to the exact card id, revision and hash.
- **WO-3:** sine-kernel Fredholm Gaudin quadrature; Haar finite-CUE spectra,
  random circular origin and effective size including Lambda=1.57314; secondary
  Wigner surmise; analytic Planck normalizer and marginal composite AIC/BIC;
  actual-size rank-copula moving-block null with refitting; joint bootstrap of
  measured minus model-implied finite-interval exponent. Baseline diagnostics
  also compare the same fitted primary statistic with CUE-generated samples.
- Two-stage runner checks freezing before loader invocation, pinned source
  hashes, disjoint declared partitions, actual denominators, receipt identity,
  replication size and stage agreement. Stage 1 is only `preliminary`.
  Nominal results cannot produce a checked kill or survivor through the lifecycle.
- One frozen kill rule: primary-only or an explicit Bonferroni allocation.
  Monte Carlo p is `(1+r)/(B+1)`; receipts also report the one-sided 95% upper
  bound. The KS kill uses that upper bound. Exponent intervals use the declared
  component alpha, with no silent 95% substitution.

## Explicit limits and review points

1. **No inferential calibration is established by this packet.** The size-check
   implementation uses independently simulated sequences from a declared
   CUE-copula / Planck-marginal null. It checks the whole union kill rule, uses
   actual n and thinning, requires at least 20/alpha outer trials and enough inner
   replicates for the smallest component alpha, and accepts only when the
   one-sided size upper bound is at most overall alpha. Small defaults test the
   implementation only. The full nested size test is `slow`, separately enabled
   with `MVE_RUN_SIZE_CHECK=1`; it was not executed. Its conclusion would be
   conditional on that null, never transferable to zeta dependence or another n.
2. **Baseline specificity remains unvalidated.** The runner reports nominal
   Gaudin/CUE diagnostics and does not manufacture baseline-exceeding survivors
   from non-rejection. It currently returns `inconclusive` unless both stages
   support a properly calibrated kill. The card lifecycle supports externally
   established, correctly bound survivor receipts for subsequent packets.
3. **H0/H1 are retrospective fixtures.** H0 is `checked:inconclusive`; H1 is
   `checked:baseline_explained`, known, human-originated and unblinded. Neither is
   handoff-eligible. All data, script and receipt hashes are real. The original
   screenshot is absent from the supplied packet: `png_sha256: null` is allowed
   only for an explicitly retrospective card with a recorded limitation. No PNG
   hash is invented. This is an archival exception, not prospective GO readiness.
4. H0 preserves its historical invalid “KS 1% OR 95% exponent CI” rule in
   `context.original_kill_rule`. Its executable specification declares a
   **retrospectively corrected** 0.005+0.005 rule; that correction cannot turn the
   v2 receipt into prospective evidence. H1's executable rule is primary Gaudin
   KS at 0.01, with its exponent secondary. The original mpmath stage-1 data are
   not silently relabeled as the later low Odlyzko block.
5. The numerical Planck optimizer retains the regression's bounded search
   (`0.2 < a <= 40`, `T > 0.01`). The finite CDF grid must reproduce the analytic
   normalizer within 0.001, otherwise it fails closed. Exponent intervals below
   grid resolution are rejected. General analytic density normalization remains
   valid for a>0 and T>0.
6. Card/receipt inputs are local assertions, not authenticated attestations.
   Production provenance and external receipt intake remain review obligations;
   this packet adds no model calls, atlas export, relay or fleet creation.
7. Prime checks are deferred, as the second fixture specified by r6.2; only the
   spacing library is delivered here. GO1–GO4 and all E-track gates remain
   unestablished.
