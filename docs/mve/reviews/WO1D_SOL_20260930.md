## Capture timing and failure labels

The native WO-6c batch-3 trace showed `#psView` attaching about 69 seconds after `goto`, while Playwright's implicit action limit was 30 seconds. The page load took about 9 seconds. The capture now sets the page action timeout explicitly to the 150-second post-load allowance; navigation remains at most 120 seconds. Both the block and legacy single-snapshot capture paths use the 270-second snapshot deadline. The 150 seconds covers the measured 60-second control delay plus draw, export, screenshots, and blind redraw with margin. Each snapshot remains under a signal deadline of **120 + 150 = 270 seconds**, or less if the pass has less time remaining. Browser launch has an explicit 60-second limit. No retry is added.

For ten snapshots, the minimum pass limit is `10 × 270 + 60 = 2760` seconds, and the two-pass batch ceiling is `2 × 2760 + 120 = 5640` seconds. WO-6c uses four snapshots per pass: `4 × 270 + 60 = 1140` seconds per pass and `2400` seconds overall. Explicit `--timeout-per-pass` must remain between its derived minimum and 2760 seconds. The free-space floor remains 5 GiB; `mve/generated` remains capped at 200 MiB. A timed-out page, pass, or overall worker has the public label `page_timeout`. Unlabeled exceptions use `internal_error`; preflight refusals remain prewrite. When an attempt exists, a 0600 `traceback.txt` contains at most 64 KiB of exception type and stack locations, without exception messages or source lines. The worker also writes a 0600 `worker_refusal.json`; the parent's bounded, retained `browser.log` captures its public label. The parent relays `page_timeout` and renderer labels instead of converting them to a source pin error.
