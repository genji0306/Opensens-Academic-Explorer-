# RH Spectral Seed Pack

This pack is for operator-first and spectrum-first campaigns.

## Included Families

- Hilbert-Polya operator heuristics.
- Random-matrix and pair-correlation evidence.
- de Branges / Hilbert-space approaches.
- Xi-function constraints.
- Quantum-inspired spectral surrogates as an advisory lane only.

## Observer Guidance

- Reward self-adjoint or positivity structure only after it is linked back to
  the explicit formula and functional equation.
- Penalize spectral analogies that have no rigorous operator construction.
- Penalize surrogate spectra that are not stable under low-order perturbation.

## Main Proof Obligations

- Construct the operator or space rigorously.
- Prove the spectrum correspondence exactly.
- Exclude off-line zeros rather than reproducing statistics alone.
