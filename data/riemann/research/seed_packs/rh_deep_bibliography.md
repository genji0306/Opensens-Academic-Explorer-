# RH Deep Seed Pack — Bibliography

Extended bibliography for the `rh-deep` seed pack, adding de_branges, xi_function,
hilbert_polya, random_matrix, and mollifier_moment families to expose K-lane
obstruction differentiation in the Klein orchestrator.

---

## de_branges: Hilbert Space Approach to the Riemann Hypothesis

- Authors: Louis de Branges de Bourcia
- Year: 1986 (initial), 1992 (extended), 2004 (current program)
- Family: de_branges
- Note: de Branges proposes a Hilbert space of entire functions whose positivity
  properties would force all non-trivial zeros onto the critical line; the program
  requires establishing completeness of the associated Sonin spaces, which has not
  been independently verified.
- Blockers: positivity structure requires full function-space proof; Sonin space completeness unverified

## xi_function: Entire-Function Structure and Laguerre-Polya Constraints

- Authors: Bernhard Riemann (1859); G. H. Hardy and J. E. Littlewood (1914);
  George Polya (1927); extended treatments by classical analytic number theory
- Year: 1859–1927 (classical foundation)
- Family: xi_function
- Note: The completed zeta function xi(s) is an entire function of order one
  satisfying the functional equation; membership in the Laguerre-Polya class
  (real zeros only) would imply RH, but no proof of class membership exists.
- Blockers: entire-function constraints are necessary but not sufficient for RH; Laguerre-Polya class membership unproven

## hilbert_polya: Self-Adjoint Operator Program

- Authors: David Hilbert (1900, oral conjecture); George Polya (independent
  suggestion, c. 1914); Michael V. Berry and Jonathan P. Keating (1999,
  H = xp conjecture)
- Year: 1999 (Berry-Keating formulation)
- Family: hilbert_polya
- Note: The Hilbert-Polya conjecture proposes that the imaginary parts of the
  non-trivial zeros are eigenvalues of a self-adjoint operator; Berry and Keating
  identified a candidate semi-classical Hamiltonian H = xp, but a rigorous
  quantum-mechanical realization with the correct spectral measure has not been
  constructed.
- Blockers: no candidate self-adjoint operator has been rigorously constructed; spectral measure connection to zeros unproven

## random_matrix: GUE Pair Correlation and Numerical Zero Statistics

- Authors: Hugh L. Montgomery (1973, pair correlation conjecture);
  Andrew M. Odlyzko (1987–2001, numerical verification of GUE statistics)
- Year: 1973 (Montgomery conjecture); 1987–2001 (Odlyzko computations)
- Family: random_matrix
- Note: Montgomery's pair correlation conjecture (conditionally proved under RH)
  matches the GUE statistics of random Hermitian matrices, providing strong
  heuristic evidence; the correlation structure is statistical evidence about
  zero spacings but does not determine the location of individual zeros.
- Blockers: GUE statistics are evidence not proof; pair correlation does not determine individual zero locations

---

## mollifier_moment: Selberg, Levinson, and Conrey Density-on-Line Results

- Authors: Atle Selberg (1942, positive proportion on critical line);
  Norman Levinson (1974, more than one-third of zeros on critical line);
  J. Brian Conrey (1989, more than two-fifths of zeros on critical line)
- Year: 1942–1989 (progressive density results)
- Family: mollifier_moment
- Note: Mollifier and moment methods establish that positive and growing proportions
  of non-trivial zeros lie on the critical line; these results are unconditional
  density bounds but do not close the gap to 100%, leaving the possibility of
  off-line zeros.
- Blockers: partial density results do not close the off-line gap; mollifier window bounds not tight enough
